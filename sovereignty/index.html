Sovereignty

Own what you run

Software is yours when you can run it, change it and keep it without anyone’s permission. Where it was written matters less than who can switch it off.

Owned, not borrowed. Most of the software a cluster runs is governed from the United States, and building it in Europe does not change that. What changes is who you depend on. Your supplier is a company under EU law. The images are copies you hold, and nothing needs our permission to keep running.

What EU law asks

NIS2, the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) and DORA all ask what is in your software, where it came from and how fast it is fixed. These are the CRA’s dates. Using the images does not make a product compliant; the mapping says which clauses they help with.

11 December 2027. The reporting duties have applied since 11 September 2026.
