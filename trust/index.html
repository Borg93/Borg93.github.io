Trust and terms

What your security and purchasing teams ask for before approving a supplier: how to report a vulnerability, how long an image gets fixes, the documents, and where things run.

Report a vulnerability

Write to security@placeholder.example, about an image or about this site. It is the single point of contact the Cyber Resilience Act asks for (Article 13(17)), and it is in security.txt.

  • Name the image and its digest, and say how to reproduce the problem.
  • You get an answer from a person within 2 working days.
  • We agree a date with you before anything is published, 90 days at most.
  • If the flaw is in the project’s own code, we report it to the project with you, or for you.

How long an image gets fixes

A version of an image gets fixes for as long as its project maintains that release line, and for 12 months after. Each image page states it. Under a paid plan the period is in the contract, as Article 13(8) and 13(19) of the Cyber Resilience Act ask of a manufacturer. The Act’s five years apply unless a product is expected to be in use for less, as one version of an image is.

Old digests stay pullable for 24 months.

Documents

Terms of service
What each plan includes, the 12-month term, renewal and notice.
Service level agreement
The fix times, which scanner counts, what happens when there is no fixed release, and the credit when a time is missed.
Data processing agreement
For the account and billing data of a paid plan. Pulling an image needs no account.
Sub-processors
Who hosts the registry and mirrors, and who sends the invoices, each with its country.
DORA addendum
For financial entities: the contract terms Article 30 of DORA lists (access and audit rights, termination, where the service is provided from), and an exit test you can run yourself, pulling from your mirror with our registry blocked.

Each is sent on request, before you sign anything: hello@placeholder.example.

Licences and the projects

Our build definitions are open source under EUPL-1.2. Each program in an image keeps its own project’s licence, which its SBOM lists. You may mirror and redistribute the 76 images under those licences.
